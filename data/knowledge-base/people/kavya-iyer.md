# Kavya Iyer

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Retailer Account Manager
- **Department:** Retailer Success
- **Primary Location:** Bengaluru
- **Email:** kavya.iyer@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** Retailer onboarding and tier servicing

## Responsibilities

Kavya supports approved retailers from onboarding through periodic account review. She coordinates profile updates, tier-review requests, order-channel guidance, service feedback, and communication of retailer policies.

## Contact Kavya For

- New retailer onboarding status
- Business-name, address, or contact changes
- Requests for Bronze, Silver, or Gold tier review
- Questions about retailer quantity slabs
- Repeated service issues that require an account-level review

## Tier Review Notes

Kavya collects the request and supporting history but does not change tiers unilaterally. Tier changes require commercial and, where credit is affected, finance approval. Changes apply prospectively after confirmation.

## Required Information

Include retailer ID, current business details, requested change, recent purchase pattern, expected future volume, and any related order or invoice references.
