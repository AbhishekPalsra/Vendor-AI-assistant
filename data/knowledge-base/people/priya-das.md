# Priya Das

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Quality and Claims Manager
- **Department:** Quality Assurance
- **Primary Location:** Kolkata
- **Email:** priya.das@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** Product quality and damaged-goods claims

## Responsibilities

Priya reviews damage, leakage, contamination, tampering, batch, expiry, and repeated product-quality reports. She can initiate warehouse quarantine and a wider batch investigation when evidence suggests more than one buyer may be affected.

## Contact Priya For

- Suspected contamination or product-safety issue
- Multiple complaints involving the same SKU and batch
- Swollen, leaking, tampered, or unusually odorous product
- Disputed damaged-goods inspection
- Approval for controlled disposal of unsafe goods

## Evidence Required

Provide buyer ID, order ID, SKU, quantity, batch number, expiry or best-before date, photographs, storage conditions after delivery, and whether any unit was sold or consumed.

Safety concerns should be escalated immediately and must not wait for the ordinary return window.
