## Summary

- **Job Title:** Data and Systems Manager
- **Department:** Technology
- **Primary Location:** Delhi NCR
- **Email:** ishaan.gupta@sampurna.example

## Responsibilities

Ishaan manages data integrations, account-master corrections, system incidents, access issues, and reconciliation defects between catalog, inventory, pricing, buyer, order, and shipment data.

## Contact Ishaan For

- Valid SKU missing from an integration
- Buyer tier differs across approved systems
- Duplicate or malformed order identifiers
- Price or inventory interface failure
- System-generated totals that do not reconcile with source fields

## Required Information

Provide the affected identifier, system or file, exact observed and expected values, timestamp, reproduction steps, and screenshots or sample records with sensitive information removed.

Technology can correct a data defect but cannot authorize a commercial price, credit increase, return exception, or policy waiver.
