# Nisha Sharma

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Inventory Planning Manager
- **Department:** Supply Chain
- **Primary Location:** Jaipur
- **Email:** nisha.sharma@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** Replenishment and stock planning

## Responsibilities

Nisha manages reorder planning, warehouse balancing, supplier replenishment coordination, and stock-availability analysis. She uses on-hand, reserved, available, reorder-level, and demand-history data.

## Contact Nisha For

- Repeated stockout of an active SKU
- Regional imbalance where one hub has excess and another has shortage
- Replenishment risk below reorder level
- Planning for a confirmed promotion or seasonal demand event
- Suspected inventory-data inconsistency affecting several orders

## Important Limitation

Nisha can provide a replenishment estimate, but future stock is not available stock until it is received, quality checked, and recorded at the warehouse. Estimated inbound quantities must not be promised as immediately orderable.
