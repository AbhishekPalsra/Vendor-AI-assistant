# Arjun Patel

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Credit Control Manager
- **Department:** Finance
- **Primary Location:** Mumbai
- **Email:** arjun.patel@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** Credit, collections, and payment allocation

## Responsibilities

Arjun manages retailer credit limits, overdue exposure, temporary credit requests, payment allocation, account holds, and collection escalation. He works from account-specific financial records rather than typical tier limits.

## Contact Arjun For

- Credit-limit review or temporary increase
- Payment sent but not allocated
- Overdue balance or finance-related account hold
- Request to apply a credit note to an invoice
- Disputed due date or payment term

## Required Information

Provide retailer ID, invoice numbers, order IDs where relevant, payment date, amount, bank reference, requested credit amount, and business justification. Never send full card details, online-banking credentials, or passwords.

## Decision Rules

A higher retailer tier does not guarantee more credit. Credit decisions consider open exposure, ageing, purchase history, payment behaviour, returns, and concentration risk.
