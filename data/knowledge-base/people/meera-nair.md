# Meera Nair

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Customer Support Lead
- **Department:** Customer Support
- **Primary Location:** Bengaluru
- **Email:** meera.nair@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** Customer and retailer service escalation

## Responsibilities

Meera supervises the support queue for order questions, cancellations, return requests, delivery concerns, and general policy guidance. She ensures cases contain enough evidence and are routed to operations, finance, logistics, or quality when specialist review is needed.

## Contact Meera For

- A support case with no response within the published service window
- Conflicting information from two support channels
- A return or cancellation process that appears stalled
- A vulnerable-customer or accessibility concern
- A repeated service problem requiring root-cause review

## Case Information Required

Include the existing case number, buyer ID, order ID, affected SKU, timeline, previous response, and the exact outcome requested. Creating repeated cases for the same issue may slow investigation.
