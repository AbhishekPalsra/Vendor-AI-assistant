# Ananya Mehta

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Head of Operations
- **Department:** Operations
- **Primary Location:** Delhi NCR
- **Email:** ananya.mehta@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** National operations

## Responsibilities

Ananya coordinates fulfilment performance across all six warehouses. Her responsibilities include national dispatch planning, operational escalation, warehouse capacity reviews, carrier continuity, inventory-control governance, and cross-functional incident management.

She owns the response when an issue affects more than one region, when multiple warehouses cannot meet a committed service level, or when a system problem creates a risk of incorrect allocation or duplicate fulfilment.

## Contact Ananya For

- Major multi-warehouse fulfilment disruption
- National carrier outage or capacity emergency
- Repeated service failure affecting a key retailer
- Operational approval outside standard warehouse procedures
- Coordination during severe weather or regional disruption

## Do Not Contact Directly For

Routine order tracking, ordinary cancellation requests, a single damaged carton, or invoice allocation. Those requests should begin with the relevant shared mailbox.

## Escalation Information Required

Include buyer ID, order or shipment IDs, affected warehouses, affected SKU count, confirmed business impact, actions already attempted, and the decision required.
