# Aditya Singh

## Summary

- **Job Title:** Logistics Manager
- **Department:** Logistics
- **Primary Location:** Delhi NCR
- **Email:** aditya.singh@sampurna.example
- **Coverage:** Carrier and shipment operations

## Responsibilities

Aditya manages carrier performance, route exceptions, lost-shipment investigations, delivery-attempt disputes, and transport-capacity planning. He works with warehouse dispatch records and carrier scans.

## Contact Aditya For

- Shipment with no carrier scan after documented handover
- Tracking that has not changed for three business days
- Repeated failed delivery despite correct address and availability
- Suspected lost shipment
- Carrier handling problem affecting multiple orders

## Required Information

Provide order ID, tracking number, carrier, warehouse ID, ship date, expected delivery date, destination, and the latest visible tracking event.

Aditya does not approve product-quality claims or retailer credit notes. Those decisions belong to Quality and Finance.
