## Responsibilities

Rohan leads national retailer sales and commercial account planning. He oversees key-account negotiations, sales targets, retailer growth programs, and commercial coordination between sales, finance, pricing, and operations.

## Contact Rohan For

- National or multi-region retailer proposals
- Commercial escalation for a strategic account
- Approval routing for non-standard sales commitments
- Coordination of large promotional or seasonal orders
- Disputes about a documented negotiated commercial term

## Pricing Authority

Rohan can sponsor a pricing exception, but a proposed exception is not active until it is documented, approved, dated, and entered into the pricing system. Verbal statements do not override active price records.

## Information Required

Provide the retailer ID, expected SKUs and quantities, delivery regions, requested validity period, current tier, payment history summary, and reason standard pricing is insufficient.
