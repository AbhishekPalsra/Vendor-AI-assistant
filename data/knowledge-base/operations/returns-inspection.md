# Returns Inspection Procedure

## Intake Requirements

Returned goods require a Return Merchandise Authorization or another approved reference. The receiving location must match the instructions issued for the claim.

## Initial Checks

Receiving staff record:

- Buyer ID and order ID
- Authorization number
- SKU and returned quantity
- Product and outer packaging condition
- Seal status
- Batch and expiry information
- Reason for return
- Photographs when required

## Disposition Categories

### Return to Saleable Stock

Allowed only when goods are unopened, undamaged, correctly labelled, within shelf-life requirements, and confirmed to have been stored appropriately.

### Quarantine for Quality Review

Used for leakage, swelling, tampering, contamination, batch concerns, unusual odour, or unclear storage history.

### Return to Supplier

Used when a supplier agreement and quality decision authorize upstream return.

### Controlled Disposal

Used for unsafe, contaminated, expired, opened hygiene, or otherwise non-saleable goods after authorization. Disposal evidence may be required.

## Credit Timing

Receipt alone does not guarantee credit. Credit or refund begins after inspection or another approved evidence-based decision. Quantity differences between the authorization and received goods must be resolved before final credit.

## Data Integrity

The return record should preserve the original order, line item, historical price, tax, and delivery information. A return is a new event and must not overwrite the original sale.
