# Bengaluru Warehouse - WH-BLR-01

## Hub Profile

WH-BLR-01 is Sampurna's South-region hub. It serves Bengaluru and supports approved routes to Hyderabad, Chennai, Coimbatore, and Kochi.

## Main Functions

- South-region parcel and retailer fulfilment
- Receiving and storage of ambient FMCG products
- Regional freight consolidation
- South-region returns intake when authorized
- Evidence collection for warehouse and carrier claims

## Service Considerations

Route time varies significantly between same-city Bengaluru delivery and inter-state South-region service. Standard estimates are 1-3 business days for same-city deliveries and 2-5 business days for regular regional routes after dispatch.

## Product Handling

Liquids must remain upright and separated from paper goods. Cleaning products must not share a tote or inner package with food. Heat-sensitive labelled goods should not remain in unventilated staging areas longer than operationally necessary.

## Returns

Returned goods require an authorization reference. Receiving staff record quantity, packaging, seals, batch data, and condition. Opened food, personal-care, oral-care, or hygiene goods are not returned to saleable inventory.

## Inventory Discrepancies

If system stock and physical stock conflict, the disputed units are held from allocation until review. Support should not promise stock based only on an old warehouse scan.

## Regional Contact

The South Region Warehouse Lead handles confirmed WH-BLR-01 receiving, picking, dispatch, and local quarantine exceptions.
