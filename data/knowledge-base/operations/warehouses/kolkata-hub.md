# Kolkata Warehouse - WH-KOL-01

## Hub Profile

WH-KOL-01 is the primary East and Northeast hub. It serves Kolkata and supports approved routes to Bhubaneswar, Patna, and Guwahati.

## Main Functions

- East-region retailer and customer fulfilment
- Consolidation for longer Northeast routes
- Quality-claim coordination with the Quality and Claims Manager
- Inventory balancing for East-region demand
- Parcel and regional freight handover

## Route Planning

Kolkata same-city shipments usually follow standard ground service. Bhubaneswar and Patna may use regional parcel or freight routes. Guwahati service may require longer lead time and stricter capacity confirmation.

## Climate and Packaging Considerations

High humidity and monsoon conditions increase the risk of wet cartons and packaging deformation. Paper, hygiene, flour, grains, biscuits, and snacks require particular protection from moisture. Wet or mould-suspect cartons must be quarantined.

## Quality Escalation

Repeated reports involving the same SKU, batch, leakage pattern, odour, swelling, or seal failure must be escalated. A batch under investigation may be placed on hold even when unaffected units appear physically saleable.

## Delivery Estimates

Service estimates begin after dispatch, not at order entry. Weather, road disruption, and carrier line-haul schedules can extend East and Northeast routes.
