# Mumbai Warehouse - WH-MUM-01

## Hub Profile

WH-MUM-01 is the primary West-region hub. It supports Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Ahmedabad, Surat, Indore, and approved surrounding routes.

## Main Functions

- High-volume retailer fulfilment
- Parcel, multi-carton, and regional freight dispatch
- West-region inventory replenishment
- Coordination with Finance for held high-value orders
- Inter-warehouse support for Jaipur and Bengaluru routes

## Delivery Characteristics

Same-city shipments are normally planned for 1-3 business days after dispatch. Regional routes commonly require 2-5 business days, while freight schedules depend on order size, destination access, and carrier capacity.

## Receiving Appointments

Pallet and high-volume deliveries require a confirmed receiving appointment. Drivers must provide a shipment or purchase reference. Unscheduled freight may be delayed or refused when dock capacity is unavailable.

## Storage and Segregation

Food products, beverages, cleaning chemicals, personal-care items, and hygiene products are stored in distinct zones. Spill-damaged cartons are isolated. Heavy goods must not be stacked on crush-sensitive paper, snack, or biscuit cartons.

## Order Exceptions

Address access, local vehicle restrictions, monsoon disruption, and freight capacity can affect delivery estimates. Support should communicate revised estimates as estimates, not guarantees.

## Finance Holds

Warehouse staff cannot release an order placed on financial hold. Release requires a system status change or documented Finance approval tied to the order.
