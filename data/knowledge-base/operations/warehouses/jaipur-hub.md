# Jaipur Warehouse - WH-JAI-01

## Hub Profile

WH-JAI-01 serves Jaipur and selected Rajasthan routes. It also supports inventory planning for North and West movements when stock balancing is approved.

## Main Functions

- Regional retailer fulfilment
- Standard ground parcel dispatch
- Storage of ambient packaged food and household categories
- Inventory cycle counting and reorder monitoring
- Controlled transfers to or from Delhi NCR and Mumbai hubs

## Service Area

Primary coverage includes Jaipur and approved urban Rajasthan postal codes. Remote or restricted destinations require a serviceability check and may use regional freight instead of parcel delivery.

## Dispatch Guidance

Orders confirmed before the local cutoff are considered for same-day allocation, subject to available stock and capacity. Carrier collection is not guaranteed merely because allocation completed.

## Receiving and Quality

Receiving staff check SKU, pack count, visible damage, seals, batch information, and shelf life where applicable. Torn food packages, leaking liquids, wet cartons, pest evidence, or unreadable labels are quarantined rather than added to saleable stock.

## Inventory Control

Available quantity is calculated after reservations. A physical count difference must be investigated before promising the disputed units. Inter-warehouse transfer stock remains unavailable at the destination until receipt is completed.

## Escalation

Regional availability and replenishment concerns may be routed to the Inventory Planning Manager. Claims involving damage after carrier handover follow the damaged-goods and carrier investigation procedures.
