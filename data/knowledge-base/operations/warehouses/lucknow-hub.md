# Lucknow Warehouse - WH-LKO-01

## Hub Profile

WH-LKO-01 serves Lucknow, Kanpur, and selected Uttar Pradesh routes. It supports North-region availability and may receive replenishment from Delhi NCR or other approved hubs.

## Main Functions

- Local and regional retailer fulfilment
- Standard parcel and multi-carton dispatch
- Inventory counts and reservation review
- North-region transfer receiving
- Authorized return intake

## Delivery Operations

Same-city delivery commonly requires 1-3 business days after dispatch. Kanpur and other regional routes usually require 2-5 business days, subject to carrier schedules and destination access.

## Stock Reservations

Reserved stock is not available to a new buyer even when it remains physically in the warehouse. Reservations may be released only by the order workflow or an authorized cancellation process.

## Receiving Transfers

Stock sent from another warehouse is in transit and must not be counted as available at WH-LKO-01 until it is received, inspected, and posted. Damaged transfer goods are quarantined and reported against the transfer reference.

## Order Holds

Warehouse staff follow system holds for credit, address, compliance, or product-quality review. A hold must be cleared by the responsible function before picking continues.
