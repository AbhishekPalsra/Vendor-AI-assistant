# Delhi NCR Warehouse - WH-DEL-01

## Hub Profile

WH-DEL-01 is Sampurna's primary North-region fulfilment and coordination hub. It serves Delhi and supported National Capital Region postal codes and may support inter-region movements when another hub requires replenishment.

## Main Functions

- Receiving packaged FMCG goods from approved suppliers
- Storage and replenishment for food, beverages, personal care, home cleaning, and hygiene products
- Parcel and retailer-order picking
- Regional freight consolidation
- Inter-warehouse transfer coordination
- Support for national logistics incidents

## Standard Operating Window

- Receiving: Monday-Saturday, 07:00-17:00 local time
- Order allocation consideration: confirmed orders received before 12:00
- Carrier handover: depends on route and daily capacity
- Closed: Sundays and notified holidays, except approved emergency activity

## Primary Service Areas

- Delhi
- Serviceable Delhi NCR postal codes
- Chandigarh through scheduled regional routes
- Selected North-region freight routes

## Storage Notes

Food and beverages must remain segregated from home-cleaning chemicals. Liquids are stored upright in designated zones. Paper and hygiene goods are protected from moisture and crushing. Temperature-sensitive instructions printed on manufacturer labels override general ambient storage guidance.

## Order Routing

WH-DEL-01 should be selected only when the required SKU has positive available quantity and the route is serviceable. A low reorder level does not itself prevent allocation, but planners may restrict stock during a confirmed shortage.

## Exceptions

Delayed carrier pickup, inventory discrepancy, damaged outbound cartons, or an inability to meet an allocated quantity must be recorded against the shipment or order. Future inbound stock should not be represented as currently available.
