# Inter-Warehouse Transfer Procedure

## Purpose

Transfers move stock between Sampurna warehouses to address regional demand, replenishment, excess, or operational disruption.

## Approval

The transfer request identifies source and destination warehouses, SKUs, quantities, reason, required date, and planner approval. The source must retain enough stock for its confirmed demand.

## Source Processing

Source staff verify SKU, quantity, batch, shelf life, packaging, and destination. Transfer stock is picked, packed, labelled, and dispatched against a transfer reference.

## Inventory States

- Before dispatch, stock remains at the source.
- After dispatch, it becomes in-transit transfer stock.
- It is not destination availability while in transit.
- After destination receipt and inspection, accepted quantity is posted at the destination.

## Destination Receiving

Destination staff compare transfer reference, seal or carton count, SKU, quantity, batch, dates, and condition. Shortage or damage is recorded before receipt completion.

## Variances

Source dispatch and destination receipt differences require investigation. Do not force both records to match by changing one without evidence.

## Quality

Goods damaged in transfer are quarantined. Short-dated batches must meet destination and buyer requirements. A batch hold travels with the stock and cannot be removed by transfer.

## Performance

Monitor transfer lead time, damage, variance, emergency transfer frequency, cost, and whether transfers reduce customer stockouts without creating source shortages.
