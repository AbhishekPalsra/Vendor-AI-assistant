# Cycle Counts and Inventory Control

## Purpose

Cycle counting compares system quantity with physical stock without waiting for a full annual count. It helps detect receiving, picking, damage, reservation, transfer, and data-entry errors.

## Count Preparation

- Define warehouse, zone, SKU list, and cutoff time.
- Pause or control movements in the count area.
- Separate saleable, reserved, damaged, returned, and quarantined stock.
- Ensure in-transit transfers are not counted at both locations.

## Counting

Count sellable units using the SKU and pack definition. Do not combine different pack sizes. Record unopened cases and loose sellable units according to the approved unit conversion.

## Variance Review

Investigate recent receipts, picks, cancellations, returns, transfers, damage records, and unit-of-measure errors. A recount should be independent when practical.

## Adjustments

Inventory adjustments require reason, quantity, timestamp, location, and approver. Material or repeated variances require root-cause analysis.

## Reservation Reconciliation

Physical stock can match on hand while available stock is wrong because reservations are stale. Review both physical quantity and reservation records.

## Quarantine

Quarantined goods remain physically present but unavailable. Count them separately and do not resolve a saleable shortage by moving them without Quality approval.

## Reporting

Track variance units and value, affected SKUs, recurring zones, suspected causes, completed corrections, and preventive actions.
