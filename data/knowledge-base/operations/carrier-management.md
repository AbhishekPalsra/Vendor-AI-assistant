# Carrier Management

## Carrier Selection

Carriers are selected based on serviceability, shipment size, product restrictions, delivery target, cost, capacity, and performance. A carrier name in historical data does not guarantee current availability.

## Shipment Handover

Warehouse staff record shipment reference, tracking number, carrier, carton or pallet count, weight when required, handover timestamp, and any seal or manifest information.

## Tracking Events

Carrier events can include picked up, in transit, at facility, out for delivery, delivery attempted, delivered, exception, and return to origin. Event timing may lag physical movement.

## Performance Measures

- Pickup reliability
- On-time delivery
- Damage and loss rate
- First-attempt delivery success
- Tracking completeness
- Claim response time
- Cost by route and shipment type

## Exceptions

A shipment with no initial scan after handover requires manifest verification. No movement for three business days may require carrier investigation. Marked-delivered disputes require proof-of-delivery review.

## Carrier Claims

Claims should preserve packaging photographs, shipment label, manifest, proof of handover, damage notation, proof of value, and buyer report. Buyer resolution and carrier reimbursement are related but separate processes.

## Changes

Changing carrier or method after confirmation may alter tracking, delivery estimate, freight, and serviceability. The buyer should be notified when impact is material.

## Confidentiality

Carrier portals and credentials are restricted. Support may share buyer-relevant tracking information but not internal carrier rates or credentials.
