# Address Validation and Serviceability

## Required Address Elements

A delivery address should include recipient or business name, building or street details, locality, city, state, postal code, and reachable contact information. Freight deliveries may also require dock access and receiving hours.

## Validation

Address validation confirms completeness and format but does not itself prove carrier serviceability. Serviceability depends on postal code, product, shipment size, method, and current carrier coverage.

## City and Postal Code Conflict

If city, state, and postal code conflict, pause confirmation and request correction. Do not choose the location that appears most likely.

## Restricted Destinations

Delivery may be restricted for remote areas, institutional security zones, vehicle-restricted markets, unsafe routes, unsupported temperature-control needs, or locations that cannot accept the shipment size.

## Freight Access

Retailers receiving pallets or many cartons should disclose access limitations, unloading capability, appointment needs, and floor or lift restrictions.

## Address Changes

Before allocation, an address change triggers new serviceability and routing checks. After shipment, a change depends on carrier rules and may incur delay or cost.

## Failed Delivery

When failure results from incorrect or inaccessible address information, re-delivery or return freight may be charged. Support should record the carrier reason and corrected details.

## Privacy

Delivery addresses and contacts are account information. Share them only with authorized staff, the relevant buyer, and service providers that require them for fulfilment.
