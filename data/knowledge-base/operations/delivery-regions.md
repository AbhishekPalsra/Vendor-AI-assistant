# Delivery Regions

## Service Model

Sampurna serves selected Indian cities through six warehouse hubs. Coverage is determined by postal code, carrier availability, order size, and product-handling requirements. A city listed below is a primary service area, but every address must still pass a postal-code serviceability check.

## North Region

### Delhi NCR Hub - WH-DEL-01

Primary service areas:

- Delhi
- Chandigarh
- Nearby serviceable National Capital Region postal codes

### Jaipur Hub - WH-JAI-01

Primary service areas:

- Jaipur
- Selected Rajasthan urban routes

### Lucknow Hub - WH-LKO-01

Primary service areas:

- Lucknow
- Kanpur
- Selected Uttar Pradesh routes

## Standard Delivery Estimates

- Same-city parcel delivery: typically 1-3 business days after dispatch
- Regional delivery: typically 2-5 business days after dispatch
- Inter-region delivery: typically 4-8 business days after dispatch
- Freight and remote-area delivery: quoted at order confirmation

## Restricted or Unsupported Deliveries

Delivery may be unavailable for remote postal codes, restricted zones, unsafe routes, locations requiring unsupported temperature control, or addresses that cannot accept the shipment size. Support should verify serviceability before promising a delivery date.
