# Shipping Rules

## Order Processing

Orders are routed to a warehouse based on SKU availability, delivery destination, product-handling requirements, and carrier capacity. A single order may be split into multiple shipments when items are stocked in different warehouses.

## Dispatch Cutoffs

- Orders confirmed before 12:00 local warehouse time are normally considered for same-day allocation.
- Orders confirmed after 12:00 are normally allocated on the next business day.
- Same-day allocation does not guarantee same-day carrier pickup.
- Sundays, public holidays, inventory counts, and severe weather may change cutoff service.

## Shipping Methods

- **Standard Ground:** Routine parcel or carton delivery
- **Express:** Faster service for eligible routes and products
- **Regional Freight:** Pallet, high-volume, or multi-carton retailer orders

The available method depends on destination, shipment size, product type, and carrier coverage.

## Packaging

- Food, beverage, cleaning, and personal-care products are separated where necessary.
- Liquids are upright, sealed, and contained to reduce leakage risk.
- Heavy cartons are not placed on crush-sensitive goods.
- Tamper-evident seals are used when required.
- Shipment labels include the order or shipment reference and destination details.

## Shipment Tracking

Tracking becomes available after carrier handover. Tracking events are carrier-provided and may not update immediately. The order status and shipment status should be treated as separate fields.

## Delivery Attempts

Carriers normally make up to two delivery attempts. Additional attempts or address corrections may incur a charge. Retailers should ensure an authorized receiver and unloading access are available during the agreed window.

## Split Shipments and Substitutions

- Split shipment may occur when no single warehouse can fulfil all items.
- No SKU substitution is made without buyer approval.
- A different batch of the same SKU is not considered a substitution if it meets the agreed shelf-life requirement.

## Delays

Estimated delivery dates are not guarantees. Delays may result from weather, road restrictions, carrier capacity, incorrect addresses, public holidays, or force majeure. Sampurna will provide a revised estimate when reliable information is available.
