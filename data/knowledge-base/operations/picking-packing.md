# Picking and Packing Standards

## Picking Controls

Pickers must use the assigned warehouse, order ID, SKU, and requested quantity. Product name alone is not sufficient because similar products may have different pack sizes or variants.

## Verification

Before packing, verify:

- SKU and product description
- Ordered and picked quantities
- Packaging condition
- Batch and expiry information where applicable
- Required shelf-life threshold
- Any order-specific handling note

## First-Expiry, First-Out

Dated products are normally picked using First-Expiry, First-Out, provided the selected batch meets the buyer's agreed remaining shelf life. A newer batch may be selected when the older batch is on hold or below the required threshold.

## Separation Rules

- Food must be protected from cleaning chemicals.
- Leaking or damaged goods must not be packed.
- Liquids should remain upright and use secondary containment where needed.
- Heavy units should be placed below crush-sensitive cartons.
- Open hygiene or personal-care packs must not be shipped.

## Substitutions

Warehouse staff may not substitute a different SKU, brand, pack size, or variant without buyer approval. A different acceptable batch of the same SKU is not a substitution.

## Packing Evidence

High-value, fragile, claim-prone, or disputed shipments may require carton count, weight, seal number, or packing photographs. Evidence should be linked to the shipment reference.

## Short Picks

If the full allocated quantity cannot be picked, staff should record the actual quantity and reason. The order must be repriced when the change affects a quantity slab. The buyer should receive a revised confirmation before dispatch when required.
