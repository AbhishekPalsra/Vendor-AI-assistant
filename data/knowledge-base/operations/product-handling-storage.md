# Product Handling and Storage Information

## General Rules

- Follow the manufacturer label when it is stricter than this guide.
- Store products in clean, dry, pest-controlled areas away from direct sunlight.
- Keep cartons off the floor and away from walls to allow inspection and airflow.
- Use First-Expiry, First-Out for dated products.
- Do not sell or ship products with broken seals, leakage, swelling, contamination, or unreadable batch details.
- Record batch and expiry information during quality investigations and returns.

## Ambient Food Products

Rice, flour, pulses, spices, sugar, salt, biscuits, snacks, noodles, sauces, cereals, and shelf-stable dairy products should generally be stored in a cool, dry area at 15-30°C unless the label states otherwise.

- Protect from moisture, pests, and strong odours.
- Resealable bulk stock must remain closed between handling activities.
- Do not place food cartons directly beside cleaning chemicals.
- Inspect flour, grains, and pulses for torn packs or pest activity.

## Edible Oils, Sauces, and Liquid Foods

- Keep containers upright.
- Avoid direct sunlight and excessive heat.
- Segregate leaking containers immediately.
- Do not use damaged caps, broken induction seals, or swollen containers.

## Beverages and Packaged Water

- Store away from heat, chemicals, and strong odours.
- Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.
- Do not freeze unless the label permits it.
- Use stable stacking heights to prevent bottle and carton deformation.

## Personal Care, Hair Care, and Oral Care

- Store in original sealed packaging in a dry ambient area.
- Keep aerosols or specially labelled products away from ignition sources.
- Segregate leaking products from food and saleable stock.
- Do not return opened products to saleable inventory.

## Home-Cleaning Products

- Store separately from food, beverages, and personal-care items.
- Keep containers upright with closures secured.
- Follow label warnings for ventilation, incompatibility, and protective equipment.
- Never decant products into food or beverage containers.
- Isolate spills and use the manufacturer-recommended cleanup method.

## Paper and Hygiene Products

- Keep dry and protected from dust, pests, and compression.
- Do not store beneath liquids or cleaning products.
- Damaged or opened hygiene packs must not be returned to saleable stock.

## Receiving Checks

At receipt, verify:

- SKU and quantity
- Packaging and seal condition
- Batch and expiry information where present
- Evidence of moisture, leakage, contamination, or pest activity
- Delivery temperature when a product label requires temperature control

Suspect goods must be quarantined and reported under the damaged-goods policy.
