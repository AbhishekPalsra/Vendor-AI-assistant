# Promotions and Bulk Orders

## Planning Requirements

Promotional and bulk orders can create unusual demand, warehouse workload, carrier capacity, and credit exposure. Early planning is required when expected quantity is materially above normal purchasing patterns.

## Information Needed

- Retailer ID and tier
- Campaign or business purpose
- SKUs and quantities
- Required delivery locations and dates
- Acceptable split shipments
- Shelf-life requirements
- Packaging or labelling needs
- Payment and credit plan
- Cancellation or return conditions

## Stock

A forecast does not reserve inventory. Stock is reserved only through the approved process. Large demand may require supplier purchase or inter-warehouse transfer.

## Pricing

Standard retailer slabs remain applicable unless an approved promotional or contract price exists. An exception requires SKU scope, quantity, buyer or group, validity dates, and approval.

## Fulfilment

Operations reviews picking capacity, packing materials, pallet or carton configuration, route capacity, delivery appointments, and split-shipment plan.

## Credit

An order exceeding available credit requires prepayment, temporary limit approval, or another documented arrangement. Tier alone does not authorize exposure.

## Changes and Cancellation

Specially procured, labelled, bundled, or reserved stock may have restricted cancellation and return rights. Conditions should be agreed before commitment.

## Post-Event Review

Review forecast accuracy, fill rate, delivery performance, damage, returns, unsold stock risk, pricing, and payment outcome to improve future planning.
