# Damaged-Goods Policy

## What Counts as Damaged Goods

This policy covers products that arrive with:

- Broken, crushed, torn, leaking, swollen, or visibly contaminated packaging
- Missing seals or evidence of tampering
- Water, heat, pest, or impact damage
- Incorrect goods or material quantity shortages
- A batch or expiry issue that makes the goods unsuitable for sale or use

## Reporting Deadline

Damage, shortage, leakage, or tampering must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Concealed damage found inside an intact outer carton should be reported immediately after discovery and within 5 calendar days of delivery.

## Evidence Required

- Buyer ID and order ID
- SKU and affected quantity
- Clear photographs of the product, outer carton, inner packaging, and shipping label
- Batch number and expiry or best-before date where visible
- Delivery receipt with a damage notation when damage was visible at handover
- A short description of how and when the damage was discovered

## Immediate Actions

- Do not sell, consume, or distribute suspect goods.
- Segregate affected stock from saleable inventory.
- Preserve original packaging, labels, and seals.
- For leaking or contaminated products, use safe containment and follow label precautions.
- Do not dispose of goods until the quality team provides instructions, unless there is an immediate safety risk.

## Resolution Options

After review, Sampurna may provide:

- Replacement stock
- A full or partial credit note
- Refund to the original payment method
- Pickup and inspection
- Authorized disposal with evidence
- Rejection of the claim when evidence shows post-delivery mishandling or an excluded condition

## Carrier Damage

Visible transit damage should be recorded on the proof of delivery before acceptance whenever possible. Accepting a shipment without notation does not automatically invalidate a claim, but photographs and prompt reporting are still required.
