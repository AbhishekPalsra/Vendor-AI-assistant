# Payment Rules

## Accepted Payment Methods

- UPI
- Bank transfer using the invoice reference
- Approved payment gateway methods
- Credit or debit card where supported
- Approved retailer credit account

Cash collection is available only when explicitly shown on the confirmed order.

## Direct Customers

- Standard terms are **Due on Receipt** or **Prepaid**.
- An order may be held until payment authorization succeeds.
- Failed or reversed payments do not create a confirmed payment record.

## Retailer Credit Terms

- Bronze: normally Net 15
- Silver: normally Net 30
- Gold: normally Net 45

The exact term and credit limit stored on the retailer account override the typical tier term. Credit is not guaranteed solely because an account has a retailer tier.

## Credit-Limit Rules

- Open invoices, dispatched orders, and approved reservations may consume available credit.
- A new order that exceeds available credit may require partial or full prepayment.
- Credit limits cannot be transferred between buyer IDs.
- Temporary increases require finance approval and an expiry date.

## Invoice and Payment Allocation

Payments should include the buyer ID and invoice or order reference. Unidentified payments may remain unallocated until the buyer provides supporting information.

## Overdue Accounts

An overdue retailer account may be placed on hold. New orders may be blocked, converted to prepaid terms, or released only after finance approval. Repeated late payment may reduce the credit limit or retailer tier.

## Refunds and Credit Notes

- Refunds follow the approved return, cancellation, or damaged-goods decision.
- Retailer claims normally produce a credit note that can be applied to open invoices.
- A credit note does not automatically settle an invoice unless it is allocated.
- Bank and payment-gateway processing times may extend the visible refund date.

## Payment Disputes

Raise invoice or payment disputes with accounts@sampurna.example and include the buyer ID, invoice number, order ID, disputed amount, and supporting documents.
