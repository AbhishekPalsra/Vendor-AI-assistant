# Pricing and Discount Policy

## Price Selection

The correct price depends on SKU, buyer type, retailer tier when applicable, requested quantity, and effective date. A pricing record is eligible only when the effective date falls within its validity period and the quantity falls within its range.

## Direct Customers

Direct customers use the standard customer price. They have no retailer tier and normally receive no retailer volume discount.

## Retailer Discounts

- Bronze: 8% for 10-49 units; 12% for 50 or more
- Silver: 10% for 10-49 units; 15% for 50 or more
- Gold: 13% for 10-49 units; 18% for 50 or more

The pricing table is authoritative when a dated record differs from this summary.

## Slab Application

Slabs apply per SKU and order line. Quantities of different brands, variants, or pack sizes cannot be combined. If quantity falls after cancellation, short pick, or return-before-dispatch, the remaining line may be repriced.

## Validity Dates

Price validity dates are inclusive unless a contract states otherwise. An order uses the price confirmed for its approved commercial event. Historical transaction prices should not be overwritten when current prices change.

## Promotional and Contract Prices

A promotional or negotiated price must identify the buyer or discount group, covered SKUs, quantity conditions, start and end dates, currency, tax treatment, and approval. An email discussion alone is not an active system price.

## Taxes

Discount is applied before tax unless the applicable invoice rule states otherwise. Tax rate depends on product classification and the legally applicable transaction context. The historical order record should retain the rate and amount actually used.

## Rounding

Net unit price and monetary amounts are normally rounded to two decimal places. Invoice-level rounding adjustments, if any, should be separately identifiable rather than hidden inside unit price.

## Disputes

Price disputes require buyer ID, order ID, SKU, quantity, order date, charged unit price, expected price, and supporting agreement. Do not promise a credit before review.
