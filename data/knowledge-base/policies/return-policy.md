# Return Policy

## Policy Summary

Returns require prior authorization. Customers and retailers must provide the order ID, affected SKU, quantity, and reason for return. Unauthorized goods may be refused at the warehouse or returned to the sender.

## Return Windows

- **Direct customers:** Request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- **Retailers:** Request a return within 14 calendar days of delivery.
- **Damaged, leaking, contaminated, or incorrect goods:** Report within 48 hours under the damaged-goods policy.
- **Short-dated goods received below the agreed shelf-life threshold:** Report within 3 business days.

## Eligible Returns

A product may be returned when:

- The wrong SKU or pack size was delivered.
- The quantity delivered exceeds the invoiced quantity.
- The item is unopened, unused, resalable, and in its original packaging.
- A quality issue is confirmed under the damaged-goods policy.
- The remaining shelf life on delivery is below the agreed threshold for that order.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Opened personal-care, oral-care, hygiene, food, or beverage products
- Products damaged by incorrect storage after delivery
- Goods purchased as final sale, clearance, or liquidation stock
- Goods returned after the applicable return window
- Products without a traceable order ID or SKU
- Seasonal or specially procured goods unless the sales agreement permits return

## Return Process

1. Contact support or the retailer desk with the buyer ID and order ID.
2. Identify each SKU, quantity, and return reason.
3. Provide photographs when damage, leakage, tampering, or incorrect goods are involved.
4. Wait for a Return Merchandise Authorization number.
5. Keep goods segregated and in suitable storage until pickup or return instructions arrive.
6. Hand over the goods with the authorization reference and supporting documents.

## Refunds and Credits

- Approved direct-customer refunds are sent to the original payment method.
- Approved retailer claims normally generate a credit note against the account.
- Refund or credit processing begins after inspection or carrier confirmation.
- Normal processing time is 5-10 business days after approval.
- Original delivery charges are refundable only when Sampurna supplied incorrect or confirmed defective goods.
