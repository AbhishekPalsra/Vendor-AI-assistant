# Complaints and Escalation Policy

## First Contact

Most issues should begin with the relevant shared mailbox: support, orders, retailers, accounts, or claims. The initial case should contain enough identifiers and evidence for investigation.

## Service Targets

- General acknowledgement: within 1 business day
- Routine order or policy response: within 2 business days
- Damage evidence review: normally within 3 business days after complete evidence
- Complex finance, carrier, or quality investigation: updated at least every 3 business days

Targets are service goals, not guaranteed resolution times.

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1 - Frontline Resolution

Routine tracking, policy explanation, cancellation eligibility, return initiation, and payment-reference collection.

### Level 2 - Specialist Review

Finance, Logistics, Quality, Retailer Success, Inventory Planning, or Warehouse review.

### Level 3 - Management Escalation

Repeated failure, material business impact, multi-region disruption, safety issue, or exception requiring management authority.

## Safety and Legal Matters

Suspected contamination, injury, fraud, legal notice, regulatory inquiry, or data breach requires immediate specialist escalation and should not wait for ordinary service timing.

## Required Complaint Record

- Case number
- Buyer ID and order ID
- SKU and quantity where relevant
- Dates and timeline
- Evidence received
- Confirmed facts and disputed facts
- Actions taken
- Owner and next update date
- Requested and approved resolution

## Communication

Do not blame the buyer, carrier, supplier, or another team before evidence is reviewed. State uncertainty clearly and avoid promising refunds, replacements, credits, or delivery dates without authorization.
