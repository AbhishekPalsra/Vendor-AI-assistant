# Retailer Credit Policy

## Purpose

Retailer credit allows approved businesses to receive goods before final payment, subject to a credit limit and payment term. Credit is a revocable commercial facility, not an entitlement created solely by retailer tier.

## Eligibility

Credit may be offered after business verification, account review, and finance approval. Direct customers normally use prepaid or due-on-receipt terms and have a zero credit limit.

## Credit Exposure

Available credit is not always equal to the stated credit limit. Exposure may include:

- Unpaid invoices
- Dispatched but not yet invoiced orders
- Confirmed orders that reserve credit
- Finance-approved adjustments
- Unallocated payments that have not yet been matched

## Typical Tier Profiles

- Bronze accounts commonly receive INR 50,000-150,000 and Net 15.
- Silver accounts commonly receive INR 200,000-350,000 and Net 30.
- Gold accounts commonly receive INR 500,000-900,000 and Net 45.

These ranges are examples. The buyer-master record is authoritative for an individual account.

## Credit Review Factors

- Payment timeliness and ageing
- Purchase frequency and volume
- Returned or disputed transactions
- Current exposure and concentration
- Time in business and verified account information
- Requested order size and seasonality
- Account holds or suspected misuse

## Temporary Limits

A temporary credit increase must specify approved amount, start date, expiry date, business reason, and approver. After expiry, the permanent limit resumes. A temporary increase does not permanently change retailer tier.

## Holds and Release

Finance may hold new orders when invoices are overdue, exposure exceeds the approved limit, payment evidence is incomplete, or unusual activity requires review. Warehouse or sales staff cannot bypass a finance hold.

## Data and Privacy

Credit information is confidential. It may be shared only with authorized staff and the relevant retailer. Never disclose one retailer's limit or payment history to another buyer.
