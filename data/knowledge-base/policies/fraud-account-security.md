# Fraud and Account Security

## Warning Signs

- Sudden large order inconsistent with account history
- Request to change delivery address and contact simultaneously
- Pressure to bypass payment, credit, or approval checks
- Multiple buyer IDs for the same business without explanation
- Unusual refund destination or bank-detail change
- Repeated failed payment attempts
- Request for another buyer's price, orders, or credit data

## Verification

Use approved account contacts and channels. High-risk changes may require call-back, document review, or management approval. Do not rely solely on the contact details supplied in the suspicious request.

## Payment Security

Never request passwords, one-time passwords, PINs, full card numbers, or online-banking credentials. Bank-detail changes require controlled verification.

## Order Holds

Orders may be held while suspicious activity is reviewed. Support should state that verification is required without revealing controls that could help bypass them.

## Data Exposure

Suspected unauthorized access, incorrect disclosure, or account takeover must be escalated immediately to Technology and management. Preserve logs and do not delete evidence.

## Refund Fraud

Refunds normally return to the original payment method. Requests for unrelated accounts or third-party destinations require specialist review.

## Retailer Pricing Misuse

Retailer pricing is for the approved buyer ID. Sharing credentials, impersonating another retailer, or splitting accounts to bypass controls can lead to suspension.

## Reporting

Record buyer ID, order or payment reference, timestamp, channel, observed indicators, actions taken, and evidence. Do not accuse the requester before investigation confirms facts.
