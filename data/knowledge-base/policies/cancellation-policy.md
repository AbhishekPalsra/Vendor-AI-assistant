# Cancellation Policy

## When an Order Can Be Cancelled

An order may be cancelled without a handling charge while it is in **Pending Confirmation** or **Confirmed** status and before warehouse allocation begins.

- Direct customers should request cancellation within 2 hours of placing the order.
- Retailers should request cancellation before the daily dispatch cutoff or before the order reaches **Allocated** status, whichever occurs first.
- A cancellation request is not complete until a cancellation confirmation is issued.

## Orders That Cannot Be Cancelled

Cancellation is normally unavailable when:

- The order is already picked, packed, shipped, or delivered.
- Goods were specially procured, labelled, bundled, or reserved for the buyer.
- A carrier has already collected the shipment.
- The order is part of a non-cancellable promotional or clearance agreement.

## Partial Cancellation

Partial cancellation is allowed before allocation when the remaining order still meets applicable minimum quantities and pricing slabs. Removing items may change the discount percentage, unit price, freight charge, or retailer slab for the remaining items.

## Payment Treatment

- Prepaid orders cancelled before allocation receive a refund to the original payment method.
- Authorized retailer cancellations reverse the account reservation or create an account adjustment.
- Payment-gateway or bank processing may take 5-10 business days.
- Orders cancelled after special procurement may incur documented procurement or restocking charges.

## How to Request Cancellation

Provide the buyer ID, order ID, reason, and whether the request applies to the full order or selected SKUs. Contact orders@sampurna.example or the assigned account manager.
