# Product Substitution Policy

## Default Rule

Sampurna does not substitute a different SKU without buyer approval. Similar name, brand, category, weight, or price does not make two SKUs interchangeable.

## What Counts as a Substitution

- Different brand
- Different product variant
- Different pack size or unit type
- Different formulation, flavour, fragrance, or model
- Multi-pack replacing individual units or the reverse
- Different SKU even when product names appear similar

## What Is Not Normally a Substitution

A different acceptable batch of the same SKU is not a substitution when it meets shelf-life, quality, and order requirements.

## Approval

Before substitution, communicate original SKU and unavailable quantity, proposed SKU and details, price and tax difference, quantity conversion, delivery impact, and return implications. Record buyer approval.

## Retailer Slabs

Substitute quantity is priced using the substitute SKU's valid pricing. Quantities from the unavailable SKU do not automatically carry over to the new SKU's slab without recalculation.

## Pack Conversions

Do not assume that total weight or volume makes a conversion equivalent. The buyer may care about retail pack count, shelf space, price marking, and consumer demand.

## Regulated or Sensitive Products

Where product regulation, allergens, safety instructions, or retailer listing requirements apply, substitution may be prohibited without additional review.

## Refusal

The buyer may decline substitution. The unavailable line can then be backordered, removed, or handled under the agreed order-change or cancellation rules.
