# Customer and Retailer Account Onboarding

## Direct Customers

Direct customer setup requires basic identity and contact information, delivery location, and acceptance of applicable order and privacy terms. Direct customers receive IDs beginning with `CUS-` and no retailer tier.

## Retailer Applications

Retailer onboarding may require verified business name, operating address, authorized contacts, tax information where applicable, delivery requirements, expected purchase pattern, and payment preference.

## Approval Stages

1. Application received
2. Completeness and identity checks
3. Business verification
4. Commercial profile and initial tier assignment
5. Credit review when credit is requested
6. Account creation and communication of terms

## Initial Tier

Bronze is the typical starting tier, but assignment depends on approved commercial criteria. Expected volume alone does not guarantee Silver or Gold.

## Credit

Credit requires separate Finance approval. A retailer can be approved for purchasing on prepaid terms while credit review remains incomplete or is declined.

## Account Data

The account should store buyer ID, type, business or customer name, city, state, contact details, tier, credit limit, payment terms, discount group, and status.

## Duplicate Prevention

Search for existing accounts using verified business information before creating a new buyer ID. Duplicate accounts can fragment credit exposure, pricing, and order history.

## Activation

Pricing and credit benefits begin after the account is Active. An application or draft record does not authorize retailer prices.

## Changes

Sensitive changes require verification and audit history. Business ownership changes may require re-onboarding rather than a simple contact edit.
