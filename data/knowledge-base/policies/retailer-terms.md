# Retailer Terms

## Account Eligibility

Retailer pricing is available only to approved retailer accounts with a valid retailer ID. The account name, business location, contact details, tax information where required, and payment profile must remain current.

## Retailer Tiers

### Bronze

- Standard retailer tier
- 8% discount for 10-49 units of an SKU
- 12% discount for 50 or more units of an SKU
- Typical credit limit: INR 50,000-150,000
- Standard payment term: Net 15

### Silver

- Established retailer tier based on purchasing and payment history
- 10% discount for 10-49 units of an SKU
- 15% discount for 50 or more units of an SKU
- Typical credit limit: INR 200,000-350,000
- Standard payment term: Net 30

### Gold

- Strategic retailer tier subject to approval and periodic review
- 13% discount for 10-49 units of an SKU
- 18% discount for 50 or more units of an SKU
- Typical credit limit: INR 500,000-900,000
- Standard payment term: Net 45

## Quantity Slabs

Pricing slabs apply per SKU and per order line. Quantities from different SKUs are not combined to reach a slab. If an order quantity changes, the applicable slab and unit price may be recalculated before confirmation.

## Tier Review

Retailer tiers may be reviewed based on purchase volume, payment history, returns, unresolved claims, credit usage, and account activity. A tier change applies only after written or system confirmation and does not retroactively reprice completed orders.

## Resale and Conduct

- Retailers must store and sell goods according to label instructions and applicable law.
- Batch numbers, labels, expiry dates, and manufacturer information must not be altered.
- Products must not be represented as manufactured by Sampurna unless explicitly stated.
- Suspected counterfeit, diverted, tampered, or unsafe stock must be reported immediately.

## Account Holds

Sampurna may place an account on hold for overdue balances, exceeded credit limits, suspected fraud, repeated delivery refusal, unresolved compliance issues, or misuse of retailer pricing. Orders placed during a hold may require prepayment.
