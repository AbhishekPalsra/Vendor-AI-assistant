# Returns and Damaged-Goods FAQs

## What is the standard return window?

Direct customers have 7 calendar days and retailers have 14 calendar days, subject to eligibility. Damage and shortage reports have shorter windows.

## How quickly must damage be reported?

Visible damage, leakage, tampering, or shortage should be reported within 48 hours. Concealed damage should be reported immediately and within 5 calendar days.

## What evidence is needed?

Order ID, buyer ID, SKU, quantity, photographs, batch and expiry details when present, delivery receipt, and a description of the issue.

## Can opened goods be returned?

Opened food, beverages, personal care, oral care, hygiene, and cleaning products are not ordinary returns. They may be reviewed when opening revealed a genuine quality defect.

## Should damaged goods be discarded?

Not until instructions are provided, unless keeping them creates an immediate safety risk. Controlled disposal may require evidence.

## Will every claim receive a refund?

No. Possible outcomes include replacement, credit, refund, pickup, disposal authorization, partial approval, or rejection.

## What if damage was visible at delivery?

Record it on the delivery receipt and take photographs before unpacking when safe.

## What happens to returned goods?

They are inspected and assigned to saleable stock, quarantine, supplier return, or controlled disposal. Receipt alone does not guarantee credit.

## How are retailer claims settled?

They normally create a credit note. Finance must allocate the credit to an invoice or account balance.
