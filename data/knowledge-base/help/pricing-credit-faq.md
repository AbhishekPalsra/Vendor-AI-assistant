# Pricing, Discounts, and Credit FAQs

## Why does a retailer need an ID for a quote?

The ID identifies buyer type, tier, discount group, payment terms, and credit profile. Without it, only a general price can be discussed.

## Do all retailers receive the same discount?

No. Discounts vary by Bronze, Silver, or Gold tier and quantity slab.

## Are quantities combined across products?

No. Slabs apply per SKU and order line.

## What happens when quantity is reduced?

The line may fall into a lower slab and be repriced. Related tax and totals are recalculated.

## Can an expired price be used?

Not unless an approved contract or exception authorizes it. Use a price row valid on the effective date.

## Is Gold tier the same as a high credit limit?

No. Tier affects standard commercial discounts. Credit is separately approved and can be reduced or held.

## Why was an order moved to prepaid?

Possible reasons include insufficient available credit, overdue invoices, an account hold, temporary risk controls, or an order outside the approved facility.

## How can a retailer request more credit?

Submit retailer ID, requested amount, purpose, expected volume, open invoice position, and supporting information to Finance.

## Does a credit note automatically pay an invoice?

No. It must be allocated to the intended invoice or balance.

## Can sales promise a special price?

Sales can propose an exception, but it becomes active only after approval and entry into the pricing system with scope and validity dates.
