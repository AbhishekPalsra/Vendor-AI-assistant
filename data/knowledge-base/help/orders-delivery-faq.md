# Orders, Shipping, and Delivery FAQs

## When is an order confirmed?

Confirmation follows pricing, stock, address, account, payment, and policy checks. A submitted request is not necessarily confirmed.

## Can a confirmed order still be delayed?

Yes. Allocation, picking, carrier capacity, weather, access, or address issues may cause delay.

## What is the difference between packed and shipped?

Packed means the warehouse prepared the goods. Shipped means carrier handover was recorded.

## Can one order have several tracking numbers?

Yes. Split fulfilment can create multiple shipments.

## Why is payment pending on a delivered order?

Retailers using credit terms can receive goods before their invoice due date. Fulfilment and payment statuses are separate.

## Can the delivery address be changed after shipment?

Only if the carrier supports it. The request may change route, charge, timing, or serviceability.

## What happens after two failed delivery attempts?

The shipment may return to origin. Additional freight or re-dispatch charges may apply.

## What should a receiver inspect?

Check carton count, visible damage, leakage, tampering, and label details before acknowledging delivery.

## What if tracking says delivered but goods are missing?

Verify address, receiver, reception areas, timestamp, and proof of delivery, then raise a logistics case.

## Is the expected delivery date guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate based on available route information.
