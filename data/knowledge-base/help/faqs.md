# Frequently Asked Questions

## Products and Availability

### How can I find products available in my city?

Provide the delivery city or postal code and buyer type. Availability should be checked against the product catalog and warehouse inventory. For retailers, the applicable tier and order quantity are also needed to return the correct price.

### Does a product shown in the catalog always have stock?

No. The catalog identifies active SKUs, while inventory records show warehouse-level availability. Available quantity equals quantity on hand minus reserved quantity.

### Can one order use stock from multiple warehouses?

Yes. The order may be split when no single warehouse can fulfil every line.

## Pricing

### How is my price selected?

Direct customers receive the standard customer price. Retailer prices depend on retailer tier, SKU quantity slab, and pricing validity dates.

### What retailer quantity slabs are used?

Retailer pricing currently uses 10-49 units and 50 or more units per SKU. Quantities from different SKUs are not combined.

### Why did my unit price change after editing an order?

Changing quantity may move an item into a different slab. Price can also change when a pricing validity period expires or an account tier changes.

### Are taxes included in the displayed unit price?

Unless explicitly labelled otherwise, unit prices are shown before applicable tax. The order calculation displays taxable value, tax rate, tax amount, and final line total.

## Orders and Delivery

### How do I cancel an order?

Contact orders@sampurna.example with the buyer ID and order ID. Cancellation is normally available only before warehouse allocation.

### How do I track a shipment?

Use the tracking number created after carrier handover. An order can contain more than one shipment and therefore more than one tracking event stream.

### What if my shipment is late?

Check the latest tracking event, then contact support with the order ID and tracking number. Support can confirm whether the delay is with allocation, carrier pickup, transit, or address resolution.

### What should I do if goods arrive damaged?

Record visible damage on the delivery receipt, take photographs, segregate the goods, and report the issue within 48 hours. Do not sell or consume suspect goods.

## Retailer Accounts

### How do I become a retailer?

Submit business and contact details for verification. Retailer pricing begins only after an approved retailer ID and tier are assigned.

### How are Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers determined?

Tier reviews consider purchase volume, payment history, account activity, returns, credit usage, and compliance. Tier changes require confirmation and do not reprice completed orders.

### Can I order above my credit limit?

The portion above available credit normally requires prepayment or finance approval.

### Why is my retailer account on hold?

Common reasons include overdue invoices, exceeded credit, unresolved compliance issues, suspected fraud, or repeated delivery refusal. Contact accounts@sampurna.example for the account-specific reason.

## Returns and Payments

### How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds normally begin processing within 5-10 business days. The bank or payment provider may take additional time to display the credit.

### Do retailers receive refunds or credit notes?

Approved retailer claims normally create account credit notes. Finance may issue a refund when required by the approved resolution.

### What information should I include when contacting support?

Include the buyer ID, order ID, SKU, affected quantity, delivery location, and a concise description. For damage claims, include photographs, batch details, and the delivery receipt when available.
