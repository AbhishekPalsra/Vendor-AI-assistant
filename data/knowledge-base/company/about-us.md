# About Us

## Company Summary

Sampurna Wholesale Distribution Pvt. Ltd. is a synthetic Indian FMCG distributor created for the vendor-assistant project. The company supplies packaged food, beverages, personal-care products, home-cleaning products, and hygiene essentials to retailers and direct customers.

Sampurna combines a central product catalog with warehouse-level inventory, customer-specific pricing, order processing, and delivery support. Retailer accounts receive tier-based prices and payment terms, while direct customers purchase at standard consumer prices.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday consumer goods reliably available to retailers and customers through transparent pricing, dependable fulfilment, and responsive service.

## What We Supply

The active catalog contains 400 SKUs across 20 categories, including:

- Rice, grains, flour, pulses, edible oils, spices, sugar, and salt
- Tea, coffee, biscuits, snacks, noodles, sauces, and breakfast cereals
- Shelf-stable dairy products, soft drinks, juices, and packaged water
- Personal care, hair care, oral care, home cleaning, paper, and hygiene products

## Who We Serve

- Independent kirana and general stores
- Supermarkets and neighbourhood retail chains
- Institutional and wholesale buyers
- Direct customers in supported delivery regions

## Distribution Network

Sampurna operates through six regional warehouses serving North, West, South, and East India. Orders are routed according to delivery location, stock availability, product-handling requirements, and service level.

## Service Principles

- Show current stock and applicable prices before order confirmation
- Apply retailer tiers and quantity slabs consistently
- Protect product quality during storage and transport
- Communicate delays, shortages, substitutions, and claims clearly
- Maintain traceable order, payment, shipment, and delivery records
