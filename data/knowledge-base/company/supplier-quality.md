# Supplier and Inbound Quality Management

## Supplier Approval

Products should be purchased only from approved suppliers or authorized channels. Approval may consider legal status, product authorization, quality systems, delivery capability, traceability, commercial terms, and performance history.

## Purchase and Receiving References

Every inbound delivery should carry an approved purchase or transfer reference. Receiving staff compare the expected supplier, SKU, quantity, pack configuration, and destination warehouse with delivered goods.

## Inbound Inspection

Inspection may include:

- Carton and pallet condition
- SKU and quantity verification
- Manufacturer seals and labels
- Batch and date codes
- Remaining shelf life
- Leakage, swelling, moisture, infestation, odour, or tampering
- Temperature evidence when the label requires controlled conditions

## Acceptance Outcomes

Goods can be accepted, accepted with documented variance, partially rejected, or quarantined. A delivery should not be fully posted as saleable when material inspection remains unresolved.

## Supplier Non-Conformance

A non-conformance record identifies supplier, purchase reference, warehouse, SKU, batch, quantity, defect, evidence, immediate containment, and requested resolution. Repeated issues may trigger corrective action, additional inspection, commercial review, or supplier suspension.

## Traceability

Batch and supplier records should enable the business to identify affected warehouse stock and delivered orders when a quality concern emerges. Product name alone is insufficient for batch investigation.

## Recalls and Market Actions

A manufacturer or regulatory recall requires immediate escalation. Affected stock is blocked, buyers are identified through transaction history, and communication follows the approved recall plan. Ordinary return windows do not limit a safety recall.

## Supplier Credits

Supplier reimbursement is separate from the buyer's approved resolution. A buyer claim should not be delayed solely because the upstream supplier has not yet issued credit.
