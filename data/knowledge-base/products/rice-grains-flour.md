# Rice, Grains, Flour, and Atta

## Category Scope

This guide covers Rice & Grains and Flour & Atta products, including basmati rice, sona masoori rice, brown rice, jeera rice, kolam rice, whole-wheat atta, multigrain atta, besan, maida, and sooji.

## Common Pack Formats

Products may be sold in g or kg units. The pack size is part of the SKU identity. A 1 kg item and a 5 kg item are different SKUs and cannot be substituted solely because the product name is similar.

## Storage

- Store in a clean, dry, pest-controlled ambient zone.
- Keep cartons and sacks off the floor and away from damp walls.
- Protect from roof leakage, condensation, and strong odours.
- Maintain intact manufacturer packaging until sale or approved handling.
- Use First-Expiry, First-Out when a best-before date is present.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect for torn seams, punctures, wet packaging, powder leakage, insect activity, webbing, unusual odour, staining, and unreadable batch details. Suspect packs should be quarantined and must not be mixed with saleable stock.

## Picking and Packing

Heavy rice and flour packs should be placed at the bottom of a stable carton or on a suitable pallet. Do not stack heavy packs on biscuits, paper products, or crush-sensitive goods. Protect paper or laminated bags from sharp carton edges.

## Quality Concerns

Common concerns include moisture, infestation, pack puncture, caking, off-odour, contamination, and short shelf life. Natural variation in grain size or colour is not automatically damage; compare the concern with the manufacturer specification and batch information.

## Returns

Opened food packs are not eligible for ordinary return. A suspected quality or contamination issue should follow the damaged-goods policy even if the pack was opened to discover the problem. Preserve the pack, batch code, and photographs.

## Retrieval Notes

To answer an availability question, combine the SKU catalog with warehouse stock. To price a retailer order, use the exact SKU, requested quantity, buyer tier, and price validity dates.
