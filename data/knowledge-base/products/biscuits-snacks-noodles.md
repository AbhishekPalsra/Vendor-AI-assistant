# Biscuits, Snacks, Namkeen, Noodles, and Pasta

## Category Scope

This guide covers Biscuits & Cookies, Snacks & Namkeen, and Noodles & Pasta. Products include glucose, Marie, cream and digestive biscuits, cookies, aloo bhujia, mixtures, chips, peanuts, moong-dal snacks, instant noodles, Hakka noodles, penne, and macaroni.

## Storage

- Keep dry, clean, and protected from heat and pests.
- Avoid excessive stacking pressure.
- Protect from strong odours and chemical products.
- Maintain sealed inner packs and cartons.
- Rotate by best-before date.

## Fragility

Biscuits, chips, and some snacks are crush-sensitive even when the outer carton looks intact. Heavy packs, liquid containers, and large detergent units must not be stacked above them.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect for crushed corners, torn pouches, deflated packs where inflation is expected, oil staining, broken seals, infestation, moisture, and short shelf life. Normal breakage tolerance may vary by manufacturer, but widespread crushing or compromised seals requires segregation.

## Packing

Use cartons that prevent movement without compressing retail packs. Keep sharp-edged pasta cartons away from flexible snack pouches. Separate food from cleaning products and leaking personal-care liquids.

## Quality Claims

Report stale odour, rancidity, unusual taste, contamination, seal failure, infestation, or major transit crushing with photographs, batch details, and affected quantity. Do not consume suspect goods to test quality.

## Returns

Unopened saleable packs may qualify within the standard window. Opened food is handled only as a quality claim. Clearance or short-dated promotional goods follow the agreed sale terms.

## Commercial Notes

The same product type may exist in 75 g, 150 g, 300 g, or other pack sizes. Always use SKU rather than approximate description when ordering, pricing, or claiming.
