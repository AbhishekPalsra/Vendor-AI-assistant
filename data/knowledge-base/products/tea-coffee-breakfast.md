# Tea, Coffee, and Breakfast Cereals

## Category Scope

This guide covers Tea & Coffee and Breakfast Cereals. Products include black tea, masala tea, green tea, instant coffee, filter coffee, corn flakes, rolled oats, muesli, chocolate cereal, and masala oats.

## Storage

- Store in a dry ambient zone away from sunlight.
- Protect tea and coffee from moisture and strong odours.
- Keep cereal cartons away from crushing and water exposure.
- Maintain sealed inner packaging.
- Follow First-Expiry, First-Out.

## Receiving Checks

Look for crushed cartons, torn pouches, broken jar seals, dented lids, moisture marks, pest activity, powder leakage, and missing batch information. A damaged outer cereal carton requires inspection of the inner pouch before disposition.

## Handling

Tea and coffee can absorb odours. Do not store them next to cleaning chemicals or strongly scented personal-care products. Glass coffee jars require cushioning and controlled stacking. Lightweight cereal cartons should not sit beneath oil, water, flour, or large detergent packs.

## Quality Issues

Potential issues include loss of seal, moisture, caking, stale odour, insect activity, broken glass, and short shelf life. Natural variation in tea leaves, ground coffee, oats, or muesli components should be evaluated against manufacturer information rather than assumed to be a defect.

## Consumer and Retailer Returns

Unopened, resalable goods may follow the standard return policy. Opened food is not ordinarily returnable, but a documented quality concern should be reported promptly with batch and expiry details.

## Availability and Pricing

Product names can be similar across brands and pack sizes. Use exact SKU for stock and price lookup. Retailer slab discounts apply to unit quantity, not total grams across multiple SKUs.
