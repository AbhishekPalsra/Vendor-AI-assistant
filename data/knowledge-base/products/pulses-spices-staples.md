# Pulses, Spices, Sugar, Salt, and Sweeteners

## Category Scope

This guide covers Pulses & Lentils, Spices & Seasoning, and Sugar, Salt & Sweeteners. Representative products include toor dal, moong dal, masoor dal, chana dal, urad dal, turmeric, chilli, coriander, garam masala, cumin, iodised salt, rock salt, white sugar, brown sugar, and jaggery powder.

## Storage

- Keep products dry, sealed, and protected from pests.
- Segregate strongly aromatic spices when odour transfer is possible.
- Avoid storage beside chemicals, fragrances, or leaking liquids.
- Protect salt and sugar from humidity and clumping.
- Follow label instructions for temperature and shelf life.

## Receiving Checks

Check outer cartons and inner packs for tears, powder leakage, moisture, infestation, discoloration, broken seals, and missing labels. Powder on the outside of a carton may indicate a punctured inner pack and requires inspection.

## Batch and Shelf-Life Controls

Batch and best-before information should remain legible. Use First-Expiry, First-Out. Short-dated goods that do not meet an agreed retailer threshold should be segregated and reported before allocation.

## Handling

Fine spice and flour-like powders can escape through small punctures. Do not tape a damaged consumer pack and return it to saleable stock. Heavy sugar or salt packs require stable lower-layer placement during transport.

## Quality Claims

Claims may involve moisture, infestation, foreign material, seal failure, off-odour, colour change, caking, or an incorrect product. Record SKU, batch, expiry, quantity, delivery date, and storage conditions.

## Product Safety

Do not taste or smell closely when contamination is suspected. Isolate the pack and contact Quality. If several buyers report the same batch, a wider hold may be required.

## Commercial Notes

Quantity slabs apply to units of the same SKU, not the combined weight of different products. Ten 1 kg packs count as ten units; they do not automatically combine with a different pack-size SKU.
