# Shelf-Stable Dairy, Beverages, and Packaged Water

## Category Scope

This guide covers shelf-stable Dairy & Milk Products, Soft Drinks & Juices, and Packaged Water in the current catalog. Products include milk powder, dairy whitener, ghee, shelf-stable cheese or butter where labelled, cola, lemon and mango drinks, juices, mineral water, purified water, natural water, alkaline water, and sparkling water.

## Label Priority

Some dairy products require refrigeration while others are shelf-stable until opened. The manufacturer label is authoritative. Do not assume every dairy SKU can be stored at ambient temperature.

## Storage

- Keep beverages and water away from direct sunlight, chemicals, and strong odours.
- Keep bottles and cartons upright when required.
- Do not freeze unless the label permits it.
- Protect cans, bottles, and cartons from impact and excessive heat.
- Store dairy products according to their individual temperature instructions.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect for leakage, broken caps, missing seals, swollen packs, dented or bulging cans, cracked bottles, wet cartons, heat exposure, and label damage. Check temperature evidence for any SKU requiring controlled storage.

## Packing and Transport

Bottles and liquid cartons require stable upright packing. Heavy water cases should be placed below lighter goods and secured against movement. Do not stack on biscuits, snacks, paper, or hygiene cartons.

## Quality Concerns

Escalate swelling, unusual odour, leakage, colour change, broken seal, carbonation loss associated with damage, or suspected temperature abuse. Do not open additional units from a suspect batch unless Quality instructs it.

## Returns

Opened beverages and dairy products are not ordinary returns. A quality concern must be reported with batch, expiry, photographs, delivery date, and storage history.
