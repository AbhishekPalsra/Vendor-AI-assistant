# Oral Care, Paper, and Hygiene Products

## Category Scope

This guide covers Oral Care and Paper & Hygiene products, including toothpaste, toothbrushes, mouthwash, facial tissues, kitchen towels, toilet rolls, sanitary pads, and wet wipes.

## Storage

- Keep products dry, clean, and protected from pests and compression.
- Store hygiene products away from leaking liquids and cleaning chemicals.
- Keep mouthwash and other liquids upright.
- Protect paper cartons from humidity and floor contact.
- Maintain sealed retail packaging.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect for crushed paper goods, wet cartons, torn hygiene packs, broken toothpaste seals, leaking mouthwash, missing caps, contamination, and unreadable batch or expiry information.

## Returns

Opened toothbrushes, toothpaste, mouthwash, sanitary products, tissues, wipes, and other hygiene goods cannot return to saleable inventory. Unopened goods may qualify under the standard policy if packaging remains intact and resalable.

## Damaged Goods

Wet, contaminated, torn, or opened hygiene packs should be quarantined. Do not reseal retail packaging and offer it for sale. Damage claims require photographs of inner and outer packaging.

## Handling

Paper goods are light but bulky and crush easily. They should be placed above heavy products and away from oils, beverages, shampoo, cleaners, or any product that can leak.

## Product Identification

Descriptions can be ambiguous when pack configuration differs. Always verify SKU, pack size, and unit type. For a multi-pack, the sellable unit is the defined pack, not an assumed individual item inside it.

## Consumer Questions

Staff may provide label and policy information but should not recommend treatment for dental, oral, menstrual, allergic, or other medical conditions.
