# Home-Cleaning Products

## Category Scope

This guide covers detergents, floor cleaners, toilet cleaners, dishwashing liquids, glass cleaners, and related packaged household-cleaning products.

## Segregated Storage

Home-cleaning products must be stored separately from food, beverages, and products likely to absorb odours. Keep containers upright with caps secured. Follow manufacturer warnings for ventilation, heat, incompatibility, and protective equipment.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect for leakage, loose caps, broken seals, swollen bottles, chemical odour outside closed cases, wet cartons, staining, corrosion, and label damage. A leaking chemical product should be isolated using suitable containment.

## Handling Rules

- Never decant cleaning products into food or drink containers.
- Do not mix different cleaning products.
- Do not place leaking goods in ordinary saleable returns.
- Use label-recommended personal protective equipment for cleanup.
- Keep spill residues away from drains unless approved instructions allow disposal.

## Packing and Transport

Use upright orientation, secondary containment, and separation from food. Do not place cleaners above paper goods, hygiene products, or absorbent cartons. Damaged caps or seals must be resolved before dispatch.

## Claims

Report leakage, missing seal, damaged bottle, incorrect product, or chemical exposure with order ID, SKU, quantity, photographs, and a description of containment actions. Safety incidents require immediate escalation.

## Returns

Opened cleaning products are not ordinary returns. Authorized returns must preserve labels and packaging. Unsafe or leaking goods may require controlled disposal instead of transport.

## Safety Boundary

The knowledge base provides logistics guidance, not poison-control or emergency medical advice. For exposure or ingestion, follow the label and contact appropriate emergency or poison-control services.
