# Personal Care and Hair Care

## Category Scope

This guide covers Personal Care and Hair Care products such as bathing soap, face wash, body lotion, talcum powder, hand wash, shampoo, conditioner, hair oil, and serum.

## Storage

- Store in original sealed packaging in a clean, dry ambient area.
- Protect from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
- Keep liquid containers upright.
- Segregate leaking units immediately.
- Follow label precautions for aerosols or flammable ingredients if present.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect caps, pumps, seals, bottles, tubes, soap wrappers, cartons, batch codes, and expiry information. Sticky cartons, fragrance outside a sealed case, staining, or collapsed bottles may indicate leakage.

## Food Segregation

Personal-care products should not be packed in direct contact with unpackaged food. Leaking or strongly scented products must be isolated to prevent odour transfer or contamination.

## Hygiene and Returns

Opened, used, or unsealed personal-care products cannot return to saleable inventory. An opened item may still be reviewed as a quality claim if the problem was discovered only after opening.

## Quality Claims

Potential issues include leakage, broken pump, missing seal, unusual colour or odour, separation beyond label expectations, contamination, or incorrect fill. Record SKU, batch, quantity, photographs, and storage history.

## Picking

Verify pack size and unit type carefully because similar brand and product names may exist in several sizes. A shampoo and conditioner from the same brand are not interchangeable.

## Safety

Do not provide medical advice about skin, scalp, or allergic reactions. Advise the buyer to stop use, follow label guidance, and seek appropriate medical assistance when necessary.
