# Edible Oils, Sauces, and Spreads

## Category Scope

This guide covers Edible Oils and Sauces & Spreads, including sunflower, mustard, soybean, rice-bran, and groundnut oils; ketchup; chilli sauce; mayonnaise; peanut butter; and mixed-fruit jam.

## Storage

- Keep containers upright in a cool, dry area.
- Protect from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
- Keep caps and seals intact.
- Separate food liquids from home-cleaning products.
- Use spill trays or secondary containment where operationally appropriate.

## Receiving Checks

Inspect for loose caps, broken induction seals, leakage, swollen containers, oily or sticky cartons, cracked bottles, dented containers, and unreadable batch or expiry information. A stained carton should be opened for controlled inspection before stock acceptance.

## Packing

Liquid containers must remain upright. Use dividers, cushioning, and sealed inner bags when needed. Do not pack heavy oil containers above glass, thin plastic, paper, biscuits, or hygiene cartons.

## Leakage Response

Segregate leaking units immediately. Protect nearby food from cross-contact. Do not wipe a leaking consumer pack and return it to saleable stock. Record affected SKU, quantity, batch, location, and nearby goods.

## Temperature and Appearance

Some edible oils, spreads, or sauces may change viscosity with temperature without being unsafe. Staff should not make a safety decision from appearance alone. Follow the manufacturer label and escalate unusual separation, swelling, odour, seal failure, or contamination.

## Returns and Claims

Opened products are generally non-returnable unless opening revealed a quality defect. Damage claims require photographs of the cap, seal, container, carton, shipping label, and batch code.

## Pricing and Ordering

Pack size and unit type must match the SKU. A 1 L bottle, 2 L bottle, and 5 L container are separate order items with independent inventory and pricing.
